### Product Thinking
Most projects begin because I need something myself. If it solves my problem well enough, I keep refining it until it's useful for others too.

### Interface Engineering
Building responsive, interactive experiences across web and desktop environments.

### Systems Building
I enjoy removing repetitive work. Scrapers, schedulers, databases, and small automations often end up becoming larger systems.

### Desktop Customization
I treat my desktop like software. If something feels fragmented, I usually end up rebuilding it.

### Open Source
Most of my projects are experiments. Open sourcing them makes it easier to learn from feedback and keep improving them.
